Est. Cost Basis Methodology
Rolling weighted-average entry price of outstanding short positions.
When short balance increases (new shorts opened):
  avg_cost = (prev_cost × prev_bal + close_price × new_shares) / total_bal
When short balance decreases (shorts covered):
  avg_cost unchanged (remaining shorts keep their weighted avg)
Interpretation: Price above cost basis = shorts are underwater (squeeze pressure). Price below cost basis = shorts are profitable (less pressure).
Limitations: Short balance is T+2 disclosure. Entry price approximated by closing price. Balance change is net (intraday open+cover activity is not captured). Initial cost basis assumes the first available closing price.
