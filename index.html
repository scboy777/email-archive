Short Positioning Methodology
Lot-ledger estimate of outstanding shorts' entry prices. New shorts are priced at the day's short VWAP (공매도 거래대금 ÷ 거래량), weighted by the balance change.
Cost Basis band: covers are removed under two assumptions — pro-rata (spread across all vintages) and LIFO (newest shorts cover first). The true average entry lies between the two dashed lines; a wide gap = low confidence after covering waves.
대차잔고 (securities lending balance, KOFIA): borrow builds before shorts go on and 상환 (returns) confirm covering during the T+2 disclosure blind window. Note: borrow includes ETF/arbitrage/market-making — trends matter, not levels; June/Dec rebalance and year-end dividend windows are structurally polluted.
과열 markers: KRX 공매도 과열종목 지정 (new shorting banned the following session).
Interpretation: Price above the band = shorts are underwater (squeeze pressure). Price below = shorts are in profit. The chart title shows the estimated share of the current balance that is underwater.
Limitations: balance is T+2 and reportable-positions-only (understates true shorts, most for mega-caps). Opening balance is priced at the first close. Moves >40% re-base the ledger (corporate actions). Covered-and-reshorted churn is invisible.
