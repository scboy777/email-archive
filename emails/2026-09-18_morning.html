* US MARKET OVERNIGHT
– Nasdaq jumps 1.7% as chip stocks surge; 10-year yield falls to 4.946% (wsj) Stocks rallied broadly, reversing losses that followed the Fed's rate hike Wednesday: the Nasdaq surged 1.7%, the S&P 500 gained 1.1%, and the Dow rose 0.6%. The 10-year Treasury yield eased to 4.946% from just above 5.0%, while Brent crude fell about 1% to below $105 on hopes that disruption from a damaged Saudi pipeline may be less severe than feared. Chip stocks led gains as the PHLX Semiconductor Index rose 3.1% after Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan flagged soaring memory prices; Micron jumped 5.5%, AMD rose ~6%, and Intel climbed ~8%. In a landmark move, the SEC cleared the way for trading venues to offer tokenized stocks in the U.S., potentially reshaping equity markets, just two days after the crypto-focused Clarity Act failed in the Senate. Generac shares gained 19% after striking a deal to supply backup generators to Amazon for its data centers, with initial deliveries expected to total $2.4 billion across 2027-2028; Generac also issued Amazon a warrant to buy its common stock. Separately, Mark Walter and Todd Boehly agreed to sell their combined 25% stake in Chelsea FC to Clearlake Capital for roughly $650 million each, as Walter faces a U.S. regulatory probe into his insurance business. Investors now watch for further Fed commentary and any escalation in Saudi pipeline disruptions.

• 후티반군 대표단이 하메네이 장례식 조문 후 예멘 사나공항 착륙 중 스톰샤도우 순항미사일 공격을 받았으며, 이는 예멘 정부군이 자처했으나 후티와 서방 분석기관은 사우디의 소행으로 보고 있음.
• 2014년 후티반군의 수도 장악 이후 사우디 주도로 아랍 연합군이 결성되어 예멘에 군사개입했으나, 빈 살만의 예상과 달리 전쟁은 7년간 지속됨.
• 2019년 자바라 계곡 전투에서 사우디·예멘 정부군 2천명이 후티반군 4천명에게 매복 공격을 당해 큰 피해를 입는 등, 사우디군은 압도적 공군력에도 불구하고 산악 게릴라전에 능숙한 후티군을 제압하지 못하고 있음.
