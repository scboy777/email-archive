* US MARKET OVERNIGHT
– Nvidia soars 31% as Jensen Huang reinstated as CEO; Nasdaq jumps 6.5%, S&P 500 up 4.7% (wsj) Nvidia shares surged 31% Monday after the chipmaker rehired Jensen Huang as CEO less than two weeks after he resigned amid an affair with a woman U.S. authorities believe funneled intelligence to China; his exit had triggered a nearly 28% slide in the stock. The reinstatement sparked a broad tech rally, with the Nasdaq closing 6.5% higher, the S&P 500 up 4.7%, and the Dow gaining 3%, underscoring Huang's outsized importance as the AI boom's chief cheerleader and financial underwriter. CoreWeave, the AI cloud firm 10%-owned by Nvidia, jumped 22%, while key memory supplier Micron Technology climbed 19% on renewed confidence in AI capital spending. Not everyone benefited: AI lab Anthropic, which listed shares last week, slipped 1.4%, highlighting uneven sentiment even within the AI complex. Notably, credit markets showed less enthusiasm—Nvidia's most actively traded bonds fell slightly in price with spreads widening versus Treasurys, signaling bondholder concern that Huang's return could reaccelerate the financial engineering and leverage he previously championed. Investors will watch whether the rally broadens beyond mega-cap tech, how bond spreads evolve, and any fresh details on the Huang affair investigation that could reignite governance concerns.
