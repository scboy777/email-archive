– 10-year Treasury yield hits 5.04%, highest since 2007, as Brent crude tops $108 on Saudi pipeline outage (wsj) Stocks retreated as surging bond yields and oil prices rattled investors. The 10-year Treasury yield climbed as high as 5.04%, its highest level since 2007, before paring some gains, pressuring equity valuations across the board. Brent crude jumped about 2%, topping $108 a barrel, after the closure of a critical Saudi pipeline threatened supply; Chevron CEO Mike Wirth warned global supplies are running low with no relief in sight, stoking fresh inflation fears. Major indexes closed lower, with the Nasdaq down 0.8%, the Dow off 0.6%, and the S&P 500 down 0.5%. Crypto was hit hardest after the industry's top legislative priority — a bill removing most crypto trading from securities regulators' purview — failed in the Senate; Coinbase shares dropped 10% and Bitcoin fell nearly 4% below $76,000. Adding to sector woes, exchange CoinEx announced it was shutting down, months after a WSJ report tied it to Iranian users, including some with ties to the regime. Investors now watch whether yields keep climbing toward 5.25%, oil supply disruptions persist, and crypto regulation stalls further, all of which could keep pressuring risk assets into next week.

미투온 인수 이게 맞는건가?
• 카카오게임즈가 미투온 최대주주 지분을 시가 대비 약 200% 프리미엄(주당 1만원)에 인수하기로 했으나, 동시에 훨씬 낮은 가격(2,842원)으로 제3자 유상증자를 진행해 평단가를 5,824원으로 낮췄음.
• 이는 대주주에게는 높은 프리미엄을 지급하면서 기존 주주 지분가치는 희석시키는 전형적인 인수 방식으로, 이사회가 이를 승인한 점에 문제를 제기함.
• 주가는 인수 기대감에 상한가를 기록했지만, 저자는 이런 구조가 상법 개정 이후에도 여전히 가능하다는 점에 의문을 제기하며 다른 시각이 있다면 의견을 구함.
