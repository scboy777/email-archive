– Trump threatens 50% tariffs on Canadian autos; S&P 500 falls 0.3%, Nasdaq drops 0.8% (wsj) Markets slid as trade tensions with Canada escalated: Trump threatened 50% tariffs on Canadian automobiles, auto parts and steel starting January, retaliation after PM Mark Carney vowed to respond to weekend tariffs covering roughly 5% of Canadian exports to the U.S. The S&P 500 closed down 0.3% and the Nasdaq fell 0.8%, dragged lower by chipmakers as Nvidia dropped 2.9% and the PHLX Semiconductor Index lost 2.7%. U.S. automakers with heavy Canadian exposure were hit hardest: Stellantis tumbled 4.8%, Ford fell 3.3%, and GM declined 1.1% on fears tariffs would disrupt cross-border supply chains. Separately, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced sanctions on over 60 entities tied to Iran's nuclear, missile and oil-revenue networks, though the treatment of China—which buys roughly 90% of Iran's oil exports—remains unclear. In IPO news, fast-fashion giant Shein priced its Hong Kong listing at a valuation near $27 billion, a steep discount from its earlier $100 billion peak, though it still ranks among the city's largest offerings this year. Investors will watch for Canada's formal response and any further U.S. tariff escalation, along with chip-sector sentiment following Nvidia's slide.

[매크로 공부] 채권 매입 자금 조달을 위해 약 1조 달러 규모의 재무부 일반 계좌를 활용할 수 있다는 의미.
• 베센트 재무장관이 채권 매입 자금 조달을 위해 약 1조 달러 규모의 재무부 일반 계좌(TGA)를 활용할 수 있다는 소식이 전해졌음.
• TGA 잔고는 원칙적으로 의회 승인 없이는 시중 유동성 공급 용도로 사용할 수 없지만, 만기 전 국채 조기 상환에는 승인 없이 사용 가능함.
• 실제 TGA 잔고는 1조 달러에 조금 못 미치며 일정 수준 이상을 유지해야 하므로, 전액을 채권 매입에 사용하는 것은 아니라는 점에 유의해야 함.
