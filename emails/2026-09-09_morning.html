– Dow drops 628 points (1.2%) as oil spikes past $99 on Saudi Houthi attack (wsj) Stocks sold off broadly Tuesday: the Dow fell 628 points (1.2%), the S&P 500 lost 0.6%, and the Nasdaq slipped 0.3%. Oil prices jumped after Iran-backed Houthi militants struck energy infrastructure in Saudi Arabia, hitting gas- and oil-storage facilities and knocking a major refinery offline; Brent crude futures topped $99 a barrel intraday before settling at $97.92. Adding to trade friction, new Canadian tariffs on $20 billion of U.S. goods — including steel, appliances, dairy and electronics — took effect in retaliation for President Trump's duties on Canadian products. On the stock front, Howmet Aerospace tumbled 11%, the S&P's worst performer, after GE Aerospace agreed to acquire Consolidated Precision Products, raising fears GE will bring more blade-and-vane manufacturing in-house and squeeze Howmet's business. Amgen shares sank 10%, the Dow's biggest decliner, after Novartis said its heart-disease drug pelacarsen failed a key trial versus placebo — a read-through concern since Amgen is developing a similar cardiovascular therapy. Investors will watch for further oil-supply disruption headlines from the Middle East, retaliatory trade measures, and any additional pipeline data from Amgen's cardiovascular program in coming sessions.
