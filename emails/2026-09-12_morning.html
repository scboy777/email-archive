– S&P 500 gains 0.9% despite hot inflation, 3.4% annual rate; Dell surges 12% on AI bullish call (wsj) Stocks climbed even as inflation data complicated the rate-cut picture: prices rose 3.4% year-over-year in August, matching July, while core prices rose 0.3% month-over-month, above forecasts. Traders now price an 87% chance of a rate cut next week, up sharply from 72% the prior day, betting the Fed will look past sticky inflation. Consumer sentiment soured, with the University of Michigan's index falling to 47.8 from 51.7, as survey director Joanne Hsu cited rising fuel costs and trade tensions weighing on household expectations. Oil pulled back even as geopolitical risk escalated, with Brent crude settling at $104.61 a barrel; Iran-backed Houthi forces effectively seized the Bab al-Mandeb strait, a critical oil chokepoint, and Saudi Arabia shut its East-West pipeline following strikes, raising supply-disruption concerns. The standout stock story was Dell, up 12% and among the S&P 500's top performers after RBC initiated coverage bullishly, arguing Dell is well positioned to capture AI infrastructure spending as agentic AI workloads drive computational demand. Broader indexes rallied on the improved rate-cut odds: the S&P 500 gained 0.9%, the Nasdaq rose 1%, and the Dow added 509 points (1%). Investors now watch next week's Fed decision closely alongside further developments on Middle East oil-route disruptions and any follow-through in AI-related hardware names.
