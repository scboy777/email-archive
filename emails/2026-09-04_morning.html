– Fed's Waller signals openness to holding rates steady; S&P 500 gains 1%, Nasdaq 1.4% (wsj) Fed Governor Christopher Waller said he would support keeping rates unchanged this month if August inflation data allows, easing fears that oil-driven inflation from the U.S.-Iran conflict would force further tightening. Stocks extended gains for a second straight session: the S&P 500 rose 1%, the Nasdaq gained 1.4%, and the Dow added 624 points (1.2%). Treasury yields retreated after touching multiyear highs earlier in the week, with the 10-year yield falling to 4.76% from near 4.8%, as softer rate-hike expectations reduced pressure on bonds. In corporate news, Nvidia agreed to acquire AI platform Hugging Face for $13 billion, its largest deal ever, surpassing the $7 billion Mellanox purchase in 2020; the move deepens Nvidia's foothold in open-weight AI models and sent its shares up 1.8%. The rate-relief rally lifted risk sentiment broadly, with rate-sensitive and growth stocks outperforming as investors priced in a lower probability of near-term hikes. Oil-linked inflation concerns tied to the U.S.-Iran conflict remain a wildcard that could reverse the dovish shift if crude prices spike further. Investors will watch upcoming August inflation data closely, along with any additional Fed commentary, to gauge whether Thursday's optimism holds into the next policy meeting.
