Equities slipped in response to the higher-for-longer rate narrative, with the Dow closing 0.3% lower and the Nasdaq down 0.1%. In commodities, Brent crude fell 2.6% to $102.59 a barrel as oil flows out of the Middle East showed signs of recovery, easing fears of a supply-driven inflation spike. The U.S. dollar continued to strengthen, up 1.5% against a basket of currencies in September, buoyed by rising yields and a reversal from two straight months of declines. Bitcoin, despite pulling back recently, remains on pace for its best quarterly performance since Q4 2024, up more than 40% from July to September and on track for its first positive quarter in a year. Investors will watch whether the dollar's ascent and elevated long-term yields further pressure risk assets, and whether Fed officials continue signaling a pause versus additional tightening.
