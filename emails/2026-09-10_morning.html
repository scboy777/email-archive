– 10-year Treasury yield hits 4.836%, near 3-year high, as S&P 500 falls 0.5% (wsj) Markets retreated broadly as Treasury yields jumped after the Treasury Department announced it would buy up to $6 billion of long-dated bonds, less than some investors expected, pushing the 10-year yield up 0.03 point to 4.836%, its highest in nearly three years. Rising oil prices added pressure, with Brent crude topping $101 a barrel after the U.S. attacked Iranian oil tankers. In single-stock news, Apple unveiled its first foldable iPhone starting at $1,999 at an event led by new operations chief John Ternus, and also raised iPhone prices to offset costlier memory chips; shares dipped just 0.3%, outperforming the broader market. Casey's General Stores plunged 14% after same-store sales growth missed expectations, a harsh reaction given shares traded at 33x forward earnings pre-report. Conversely, Jersey Mike's Subs, recently IPO'd, rose 7.5% on its first earnings report as a public company. Investors will watch for further Fed/Treasury signals on rate policy, oil-driven inflation risks, and how consumer-facing retailers navigate slowing same-store sales trends heading into year-end.

근원CPI가 1.8%인데도, 일본은행의 금리인상이 예상되는 비밀
• 일본 2분기 GDP가 연율 1.4%로 상향 수정되고 명목임금도 34년 만에 6개월 연속 3% 이상 상승하며, 일본은행의 9월 금리인상 명분이 강화되고 있음.
• 다만 근원CPI는 2.0% 목표에 미달한 상태인데, 이는 정부가 휘발유·경유 잠정세 폐지와 전기·가스 보조금 재개 등 정책적으로 물가를 약 0.7%p 억눌렀기 때문이라는 시각이 있음.
• 이 때문에 일부 금융정책위원들은 현재 CPI가 '정상적이지 않다'고 판단하며 금리인상을 주장하고 있어, 표면적 물가 수치와 실제 인상 압력 사이에 괴리가 존재함.
