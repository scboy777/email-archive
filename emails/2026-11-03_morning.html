* US MARKET OVERNIGHT
– Nasdaq falls 1.1% as AI selloff deepens; oil crashes 7.6% on Iran de-escalation hopes. (wsj) Markets reversed hard Monday afternoon as renewed weakness in speculative AI names dragged the Nasdaq down 1.1% and the S&P 500 down 0.5%, even as the Dow gained 213 points (0.4%) on rotation into old-economy names like Sherwin-Williams, Home Depot and Walmart. Oil cratered on unconfirmed reports that President Trump won't rush fresh strikes on Iran until after midterms, sending Brent crude futures down 7.63% to $104.77; the 10-year Treasury yield settled at 4.893%. The biggest single-stock story was Marvell's ~$80 billion all-stock deal to buy Lumentum, expanding its data-center optics business, but investors punished both sides on dilution fears — Marvell fell 13% and Lumentum plunged 19%; Nvidia is a strategic investor in both firms. AI datacenter plays CoreWeave and Oracle each dropped roughly 9%, extending the broader AI-infrastructure retreat. Separately, Anthropic's updated IPO filing revealed aggregate committed spend of approximately $895.4 billion on compute, alongside Q3 revenue of $17 billion, up from $1.4 billion a year earlier, underscoring the scale of AI infrastructure bets. Watch for further Iran diplomatic signals, AI capex sentiment, and any follow-through in bond yields and oil prices.

• 아마존도 자체 개발한 AI칩 트레이니엄으로 앤비디아 GPU 없이 앤트로픽 전용 데이터센터를 구축했으며, 트레이니엄3·4로 갈수록 성능이 엔비디아에 근접하고 NVLink Fusion 지원으로 혼용 사용도 가능해질 전망임.
• 이는 엔비디아 독점 구도에 균열이 생기고 구글 TPU와 아마존 트레이니엄 등 자체 AI칩 생태계가 빠르게 성장하고 있음을 시사하며, 삼성전자·SK하이닉스 등 메모리 기업들에게도 공급망 다변화에 따른 영향이 예상됨.
