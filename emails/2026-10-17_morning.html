* US MARKET OVERNIGHT
– Nasdaq drops 2.1% as AI-spending fears hit CoreWeave, down 11.9% on day, 23% on week (wsj) Tech led a broad risk-off Friday, with the Nasdaq composite falling 2.1% to cap a volatile week. Renewed worries over uncertain returns on massive AI infrastructure spending weighed heavily on CoreWeave, the heavily-indebted AI cloud-computing firm, which tumbled 11.9% on the day and is down more than 23% for the week as investors question the economics behind the AI buildout. Tesla also slid 2.1% after Elon Musk's unveiling of a flight-capable Roadster, slated for a 2028 debut, failed to excite investors looking for nearer-term catalysts. Treasury yields ticked higher even as they moderated overall this week on safe-haven buying; the 10-year Treasury yield rose to 5.1%, still below its recent high near 5.3%, after a Treasury dealer survey gave no signal of reduced long-term debt auction sizes, removing a potential cap on yields. Oil prices jumped, with Brent crude up 3.7% and approaching $110 a barrel, its highest since May, further pressuring yields via inflation expectations the Fed monitors. Investors now watch whether AI-related selloffs spread further through mega-cap tech and how rising energy costs and yields shape the Fed's next policy signals.
