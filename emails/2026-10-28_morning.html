– Nasdaq jumps 1.2% on AI rally; CoreWeave up 8%, Boeing sinks 11% on wider loss (wsj) Tech shares extended Monday's Anthropic-driven rally, led by chip makers and AI-infrastructure names. Separately, law firm Weil, Gotshal & Manges hired a top bankruptcy lawyer to weigh options amid deteriorating prospects, a striking development for a firm that posted $2.1 billion in revenue last year. Investors should watch oil-driven inflation risk, further AI-stock momentum, and Boeing's delivery guidance going forward.
