– Dow drops 0.9% snapping five-day streak; Datadog -19%, Western Digital -13% lead tech rout (wsj) The Dow fell 0.9%, ending a five-session winning streak, while the S&P 500 slipped 0.2% and the Nasdaq declined 0.1%. Memory-chip names got hit after quarterly reports: Western Digital tumbled 13% despite in-line guidance, and Sandisk dropped 7% on forward guidance that missed estimates, dragging down other AI-linked hardware plays. Datadog sank 19% after disclosing a major AI-company customer is cutting usage, and management's sales outlook for the year came in below Wall Street estimates despite solid Q2 earnings. Honeywell Aerospace shares tumbled 23% in its first report since June's spinoff, cutting its full-year outlook as supply constraints weighed on sales growth. Elsewhere, oil prices jumped almost 4% on Brent crude as investors watched talks to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, with Iran signaling a shipping-route deal with Oman. The 10-year Treasury yield rose to 4.68%, while gold held roughly flat near $4,304. Investors will next watch for more earnings-driven guidance from tech and industrial names, along with further developments on the Hormuz shipping talks and their impact on oil.
