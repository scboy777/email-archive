– S&P 500, Nasdaq hit record closes as 10-year Treasury yield eases to 5.27% (wsj) The S&P 500 rose 0.6% and the Nasdaq Composite gained 0.5%, both closing at record highs, while the Dow added 253 points (0.5%). Treasury yields pulled back from multi-decade highs, with the 10-year yield easing to just under 5.27% after touching its highest level since 2002 on Monday; a 10-year note auction on Wednesday will test demand further. French government bonds, under pressure amid fiscal concerns with yields near 5%, also eased alongside U.S. Treasurys and German Bunds, calming some global rate anxiety. Brent crude edged up 0.3% to $100.58 a barrel, pulling back from the $100 threshold as exporters find new shipping routes despite lingering tanker-attack risks in the Strait of Hormuz. Chip stocks were mixed: Seagate Technology tumbled over 9% and Western Digital fell almost 7% on reports of a potential bidding contest for a component maker, while Marvell Technology jumped nearly 6% after unveiling an ambitious revenue target at its investor day, helping the PHLX Semiconductor index eke out a gain. Investors will watch Wednesday's Treasury auction and further developments in French and global bond markets for direction.
