Memory makers were hit hardest, with Sandisk down another 14% and Micron off 8.9%. Software stocks staged a partial recovery as investors rotated out of chips, with Workday up 8.2% and Thomson Reuters up 5.7%, both rebounding from earlier-year AI-disruption fears; still, the Nasdaq Composite slipped 0.2%. The Dow bucked the trend, gaining 1% (537 points) on strong earnings from components Sherwin-Williams (+8%) and Coca-Cola (+5%), while the S&P 500 edged up 0.2%. Crude oil fell for a third straight session, with U.S. benchmark futures dipping below $80/barrel amid a continued U.S.-Iran strike pause and progress toward reopening the Strait of Hormuz. Visa announced plans to cut 7% of staff (about 2,600 jobs), joining Intuit, Cisco, and Meta Platforms in citing AI-driven efficiency gains for recent layoffs. Investors will watch whether chip weakness spreads further or software gains extend, alongside more earnings and AI-capex commentary.

• 이 불안정한 시스템은 예르막 비서실장의 개인 역량으로 지탱되어 왔으나, 이는 지속 가능하지 않은 구조였음을 시사함.
