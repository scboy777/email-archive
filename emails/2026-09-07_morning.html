In single-stock news, Volkswagen shares jumped 6.5% after its board approved doubling planned workforce cuts to 100,000 as part of a sweeping restructuring that will halve its model lineup, responding to intensifying Chinese competition and U.S. tariff pressures. Investors now turn to upcoming inflation data and Fed commentary ahead of the September meeting to gauge whether the strong jobs report shifts the central bank's rate-cut trajectory.
