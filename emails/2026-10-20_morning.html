– Nasdaq jumps 1.1% as tech rebounds; Cleveland-Cliffs soars 16% on upbeat 2027 profit outlook (wsj) Tech stocks led a rebound Wednesday, with the Nasdaq Composite gaining 1.1% and the S&P 500 adding 0.4%, while the Dow slipped 36 points, or 0.1%, as a pocket of weakness in industrials and defensives offset tech strength. The standout mover was steelmaker Cleveland-Cliffs, whose shares jumped 16% after management guided that 2027 profit would be the highest since 2021, citing tight steel supply conditions supporting pricing power. In a notable philanthropic headline, Elon Musk said he would gift every American child a SpaceX share, following pledges from Anthropic and other billionaires to give away wealth; based on current valuation and the U.S. under-18 population, that commitment implies roughly $10 billion in stock, a reminder of SpaceX's soaring private valuation. Geopolitical risk resurfaced as Saudi Arabia postponed its high-profile 'Davos in the Desert' investment conference, originally set for next week, until 2025, after Houthi attacks raised security concerns among attendees worried about potential targeting of hotels and landmarks near the event. The tech rebound suggests investors are stepping back into growth names after recent volatility, while the Dow's modest decline points to rotation away from more defensive, value-oriented sectors. Investors should watch upcoming earnings for confirmation of the tech recovery's durability, further developments in Middle East security risk that could affect oil and travel-linked sectors, and any follow-through in steel and industrial pricing trends following Cleveland-Cliffs' bullish guidance.
