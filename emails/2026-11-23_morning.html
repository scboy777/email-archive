– Nasdaq plunges 5.3% as AI trade cracks; S&P 500, Dow enter correction, down over 10% from peaks (wsj) The Nasdaq Composite tumbled 5.3% Friday, its worst week in over a year and now down more than 2% YTD, as the AI buildout trade suffered a major reversal. The S&P 500 fell 3.7% and the Dow shed 949 points (1.9%), both now more than 10% below recent peaks, officially entering correction territory. Anthropic was a standout casualty, popping 17% on its IPO debut Thursday before tumbling 8.5% Friday, dipping as low as $83.05—barely above its listing price—marking an unusually sharp reversal versus recent debuts. Companies tied to physical AI infrastructure were hit hardest: Sandisk, Bloom Energy, and Nvidia posted the steepest S&P 500 declines, while the PHLX Semiconductor index dropped nearly 11%, cutting its 2026 gain to under 19%. Treasurys rallied hard as investors fled to safety: 10-year yields fell 0.14 point to 4.909%, down 0.24 point on the week, while 2-year yields dropped to 4.611%, their biggest weekly decline since 2024. Commodities offered no refuge—gold fell 4.6% to $3,512/oz, hitting new 52-week lows, while Brent crude dropped 3.4% on the week to $92.57, and copper futures slid sharply off all-time highs, signaling investor concern over a slowing AI infrastructure buildout. Watch for follow-through in semiconductor and data-center names next week as markets reassess AI capex expectations.
