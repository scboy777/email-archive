– Brent hits $91 on tanker seizure fears; Dow -0.5%, 30-yr yield 5.31%, 19-year high. (wsj) Geopolitical risk and bond-market stress dominated Monday's session. Brent crude jumped to roughly $91/barrel after Iranian media reported a tanker seizure in the Strait of Hormuz, compounded by President Trump's threat to bomb Oman if it obstructs a U.S.-Iran peace deal. Treasurys sold off further, with the 30-year yield closing at a fresh 19-year high of 5.31% and the 10-year rising to 4.725% from 4.695% Friday, reflecting mounting concern over fiscal and inflation risk. Major indexes slipped despite the geopolitical and rates backdrop: the Dow fell 0.5%, the S&P 500 lost 0.5%, and the Nasdaq declined 0.3%. Weak China retail sales data (+0.6% YoY, missing estimates) added to global growth worries just days after soft U.S. retail sales, sharpening focus on upcoming earnings from Target and Walmart as consumer-health gauges. Chipmakers and AI names were a bright spot—SpaceX-linked optimism drove some AI-adjacent gains after filings revealed backers including Nvidia, Harvard, and Peter Thiel, sending related shares up as much as 4.4%. 투자의 심리
• 복잡계 투자에서는 정교한 예측 모델보다 좋은 기업을 싸게 사서 오래 보유하는 단순한 규칙이 더 강력하고 견고함.
• 매매는 뇌의 도파민 보상 시스템과 통제 환상에 기반한 중독적 행동인 반면, 투자자는 평균 수익률만으로도 충분히 성공할 수 있음.
• 매크로 변수는 예측의 도구가 아닌 침체 산업 속 우량주 발굴과 공포 시 매수의 기회로 활용해야 하며, 좋은 투자는 지식보다 의식과 관점의 문제임.
