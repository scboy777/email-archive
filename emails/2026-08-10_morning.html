* US MARKET OVERNIGHT
– S&P 500 hits record as weak jobs data revives Fed rate-cut bets; Airbnb surges 17%, Trade Desk plunges 22% (wsj) Stocks rallied after a surprise labor-market contraction, with the S&P 500 closing at a record, the Nasdaq up 1.3%, and the Dow gaining 0.3%. The Labor Department reported the economy lost 23,000 jobs in July, sharply missing expectations for an 83,300 gain, with May and June payrolls also revised lower, reviving questions about economic strength despite still-elevated inflation. Traders trimmed bets on a Fed rate hike next month, and 10-year Treasury yields initially fell before retracing most declines to 4.65%. Manufacturing was a bright spot, adding 5,000 jobs led by transportation and computer-equipment makers, extending a months-long production rebound even as factory headcount remains below year-ago levels. Trade Desk shares dropped 22%, the S&P 500's worst performer, after second-quarter revenue missed expectations. Airbnb jumped 17%, the index's top gainer, after raising its full-year forecast on stronger global demand and AI-driven platform improvements. Commodities diverged: gold rose 2.3% to $4,395.50 on safe-haven demand, while crude oil slipped slightly to $77.04. Investors now watch upcoming inflation data and Fed commentary for confirmation of a September rate cut.
